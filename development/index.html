HOW THE CALENDAR WORKS

Four weeks. Every month.

Each standard month begins on the same weekday and contains four uninterrupted weeks. The day-of-week sequence never skips a day.

01

Thirteen standard monthsMonths 01–13 each contain exactly 28 days.

02

A fourteenth month in leap yearsQualifying years include the seven-day intercalary “Hany’s Week”.

03

The Base-5 protocolAdd a leap week for years divisible by 5; skip it for years divisible by 50; also skip when (year − 25) is divisible by 400.

04

Our epochYear 2026, Month 01, Day 01 corresponds to December 21, 2025, the epoch specified in the 5YLW proposal.
